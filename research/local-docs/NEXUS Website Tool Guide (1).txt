1. Ahrefs Webmaster Tools
Focus area: protecting the old site's link value during the move · Cost: free for verified
sites · ahrefs.com/webmaster-tools
What it is. Ahrefs' free toolset for site owners, recently renamed "Ahrefs Free". It uses the
same data as the paid Ahrefs product, limited to websites you prove you own.
Why NEXUS needs it. 884 other websites link to nexuscreativehq.com. Those links are
the main reason the domain is trusted, and they point at old addresses. If an old address
simply disappears, the value of its links is lost. This tool shows exactly which old pages
hold links, including the ones already returning errors.
What it includes
Site Explorer: every site linking to NEXUS, the pages they link to, and broken links.
Site Audit: checks for more than 170 technical and on-page SEO issues, with advice on
fixing each one.
Web Analytics: a cookie-free traffic counter.
How we will use it
1. Verify nexuscreativehq.com, quickest by connecting Google Search Console.
2. Export the "best by links" list, including pages that now return errors, and add every
address on it to the redirect map.
3. Check the quality of the linking sites, so we know which links matter most.
4. After launch, run a monthly Site Audit on the new site and check that no linked address
returns an error.
Limits. It works only on websites you own: looking at competitors needs a paid plan. Site
Audit has 5,000 crawl credits a month, plenty for this site, and Site Explorer shows up to
1,000 backlinks or keywords at once.
Paid upgrade. Ahrefs Lite, $129/month, adds competitor research, rank tracking and AI
prompt checks, and connects directly to Claude.
2. Screaming Frog SEO Spider
Focus area: technical SEO checks and redirect testing · Cost: free up to 500 URLs ·
screamingfrog.co.uk/seo-spider
What it is. A desktop program for Windows, macOS and Linux that crawls a website the
way a search engine does, then lists every page with its status, title, headings, canonical
tag, redirects and broken links.

Why NEXUS needs it. Your brief's sign-off checks include unique titles and descriptions,
one H1 per page, correct canonical tags, no broken links, and a single 301 redirect from
every old address. This tool checks all of them in one crawl and produces reports we can
hand over as evidence.
How we will use it
1. Crawl the staging site to find missing or duplicate titles and descriptions, pages
without exactly one H1, wrong canonical tags, broken links and images without alt text.
2. Test the redirect map: load the full list of old addresses and confirm each returns one
301 to the right new page, with no chains or loops.
3. Repeat on launch day against the live domain, then again a week after launch.
4. Keep the exports as acceptance evidence.
Limits. The free version crawls up to 500 URLs at a time, which covers this site, but it can't
save crawls or render JavaScript, and some configuration options are locked.
Paid upgrade. A licence costs £199 a year per user. It removes the limit and unlocks saved
crawls, JavaScript rendering and Google integrations. NEXUS is unlikely to need it at this
size.
3. Google Search Console
Focus area: visibility in Google search · Cost: free · search.google.com/search-console
What it is. Google's own service for website owners. It is the only source of real data on
which searches show NEXUS in Google, how often, and how many people click.
Why NEXUS needs it. Your brief requires it, owned by a NEXUS account. Today NEXUS
ranks for two searches, both for its own name. Search Console is where we will see
whether the audience pages start appearing for searches from buyers who don't know
NEXUS yet, the main SEO goal.
Reports we will use
Performance: searches, clicks, impressions, click-through rate and average position,
for each page and each search term.
Page indexing: which pages Google has indexed, and any errors such as old addresses
returning 404.
Sitemaps: submitting the new sitemap and checking Google reads it.
URL Inspection: asking Google to crawl a key page as soon as it goes live.
Core Web Vitals: real-visitor speed data, once traffic is high enough.
How we will use it

4. Bing Webmaster Tools
Focus area: visibility in Bing and AI answers · Cost: free · bing.com/webmasters
What it is. Microsoft's equivalent of Search Console, covering Bing search and, through its
AI Performance report, Microsoft's AI answers.
Why NEXUS needs it. Your brief requires it, and asks that AI assistants describe NEXUS
accurately and send the right people to the right page. Bing Webmaster Tools is currently
the only free source showing when an AI assistant cites your pages. Bing is also available
in mainland China, where Google is not.
The AI Performance report
Covers citations in Microsoft Copilot, AI-generated summaries in Bing, and some
partner AI services.
Pages: which NEXUS pages are cited in AI answers, and how often.
Grounding queries: the phrases the AI searched for when it picked your content, with
your share of the citations for each.
Exports to a spreadsheet for monthly reporting.
How we will use it
1. Add the site by importing it from Google Search Console. Data appears within about a
day.
2. Submit the new sitemap at launch, and turn on IndexNow so Bing hears about new and
changed pages straight away.
3. Check the AI Performance report monthly: which pages AI cites, and whether it
describes NEXUS with the approved one-line description.
4. Use the grounding queries to plan Insights articles and FAQ answers.

5. Semrush
Focus area: keyword and competitor research · Cost: free, 10 reports a day · semrush.com
What it is. One of the two leading SEO research platforms, alongside Ahrefs. It estimates
how many people search for a term, how hard it is to rank for, and which websites rank for
it now.
Why NEXUS needs it. Each audience page should match what its buyers actually type.
For example, would an international company search "UK market entry", "expand to the
UK" or "soft landing UK"? Semrush answers that with UK search volumes, and shows
which consultancies, agencies and programmes already rank, so we know who NEXUS is
competing with in search.
How we will use it
1. Run Keyword Overview on the candidate terms for each audience page, using the UK
database: monthly searches, difficulty and search intent.
2. Pick one main term and two or three supporting terms per page, and pass them into
the page titles, headings and copy.
3. Run Domain Overview on the three to five sites ranking highest for those terms, to see
what they rank for and how strong they are.
4. After launch, recheck the main terms monthly.
Limits. The free account allows 10 reports a day across all tools and one project, with
results capped per report. That is enough for focused checks, so we will plan each day's 10
reports in advance.
Paid upgrade. The SEO plan costs $139/month ($117.33/month billed yearly) and removes
the daily cap. A version with AI search tracking costs $199/month. Semrush connects
directly to Claude.
6. SparkToro
Focus area: audience research · Cost: free, 3 preview reports a month · sparktoro.com
What it is. An audience research tool. You describe an audience, for example people who
talk about "UK market entry" or follow a particular account. It shows which websites,
podcasts, YouTube channels and social accounts that audience pays attention to, and the
words they use.

Why NEXUS needs it. Institutions and sponsors rarely arrive through Google. They come
through events, partners, LinkedIn and the media they already follow. SparkToro shows
where those audiences spend their time, so content, partnerships and awards promotion
can go where they will be seen.
How we will use it
1. Spend the three free previews on the three audiences hardest to reach through
search: international technology companies, innovation institutions and professional
firms.
2. List the publications, podcasts and accounts each audience follows, as targets for
Insights distribution, PR and partnerships.
3. Note the phrases each audience uses, and check them against the Semrush keyword
research.
Limits. The free plan shows preview data only. SparkToro's data comes mostly from the
English-language web and social networks, so it says little about audiences on Chinese
platforms such as WeChat. For China-based institutions, NEXUS's own partner knowledge
will be the better guide.
Paid upgrade. Business, $150/month, gives 25 full reports a month. Worth it only for a
dedicated audience research project.
7. Lyssna
Focus area: message clarity · Cost: free, 15 responses from people you recruit ·
lyssna.com
What it is. A user research tool. Its five-second test shows a person a screen for five
seconds, then asks them questions about what they remember and understood.
Why NEXUS needs it. The brief's first goal for the home page is to "explain NEXUS in five
seconds". A five-second test measures that directly, before launch, using the prototype. It
is quicker and cheaper to fix a headline now than after launch.
How we will use it
1. Upload screenshots of the home page hero and the three audience page heroes from
the prototype.
2. Ask three questions: What does this company do? Who is it for? What would you click
first?
3. Send the link to 5 to 10 people from NEXUS's network, ideally one or two from each
audience. Lyssna recommends 5 to 10 participants for this kind of test.

8. Microsoft Clarity
Focus area: visitor behaviour · Cost: free, with no traffic limit · clarity.microsoft.com
What it is. Microsoft's free behaviour analytics tool. It records anonymised sessions and
builds heatmaps showing where visitors click, how far they scroll, and where they get
stuck. Built-in AI summarises patterns across sessions and answers questions about the
data.
Why NEXUS needs it. Google Analytics shows that a visitor left a page. Clarity shows
why: the headline they skipped, the logo they tried to click, the form field where they gave
up. The approved home page has 15 sections, so we also need to know how far down
visitors actually get.
What it shows
Heatmaps: click and scroll maps for each page, with AI summaries.
Session recordings: individual visits played back, with key moments highlighted.
Frustration signals: repeated clicks on the same spot, clicks that do nothing, and quick
returns to the previous page.
AI visibility: how AI services cite NEXUS content and how AI crawlers visit the site.
How we will use it
1. Install it behind the cookie banner. Since 31 October 2025, Clarity requires a valid
consent signal for visitors from the UK, the EEA and Switzerland. Without consent it
sets no cookies and collects only limited data.
2. Keep form fields and personal data masked in recordings.
3. Each week after launch, review the home and audience page heatmaps, and watch
recordings of visits that started a form but didn't finish it.
4. Report the top three usability fixes each month.
Limits. Data comes only from visitors who accept cookies, and with low early traffic,
patterns take a few weeks to emerge. Clarity offers its own connection for AI assistants,
so its data can also be analysed with Claude. Source: Microsoft Learn, consent

has enough visitors, it also shows real-world speed data from Chrome users.
Why NEXUS needs it. Your brief's sign-off checks are written in Lighthouse terms, so this

How we will use it

staging is password-protected.
2. Fix every issue that pulls a template below target.
3. Re-test the live pages on launch day, and save the reports as acceptance evidence.
