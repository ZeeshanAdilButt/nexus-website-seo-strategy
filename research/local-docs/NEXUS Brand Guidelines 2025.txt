Colour Usage Rules
Backgrounds should be plain. Electric Yellow is an accent 
colour — not a background. Dark/light contrast must 
always be maintained for legibility and accessibility.
✓  White on Black
✓  Metalic  on White
✓  Black on Yellow (accents only)
✕  White on Yellow — fails contrast
✕  White on Grey — avoid
