The challenge

“We need international outcomes for our companies, but a UK team takes years to build.”

Innovation institutions are expected to show results abroad: companies landing, partnerships signed, investment won. Doing that from a distance is slow, and opening an overseas office is expensive and hard to justify.

NEXUS gives you an operational presence in the UK from day one, run by a team that already works on both sides of the UK and China corridor.

What we provide

Two ways to work with us, each scoped to your institution and priced on application.

Annual

UK Representation

We act as your UK presence for the year, representing your institution and your companies.

  • A named UK point of contact for your institution
  • Showcase events and exhibition presence in the UK
  • A pipeline of qualified UK partners, customers and investors
  • Landing support for companies you refer to us
  • Regular reporting on activity and outcomes

Annual retainer, on application
