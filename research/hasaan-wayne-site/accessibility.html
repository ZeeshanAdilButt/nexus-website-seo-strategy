Our aim

This website is run by NEXUS Creative HQ Ltd. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

The site is built so that you can:

  • zoom in up to 400% and pinch to zoom on a phone, without content running off the side of the screen
  • use every link, button, menu and form with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline
  • skip straight to the main content
  • use a screen reader: each page has one main heading, headings in order, labelled form fields and text alternatives for images
  • read text with enough contrast against its background
  • turn off motion: the site respects your device's reduced-motion setting

What we have tested

Before launch the build team checked each page in a real browser at 1440, 768 and 390 pixels wide, with automated checks for colour contrast, headings, focus colours, text size, sideways scrolling and broken images. We also checked the page code for headings, landmarks, labels and alternative text.

Known limitations

  • Some pages still show highlighted placeholders, such as [to confirm], where final text, figures or photographs are on their way. They will be replaced or removed before launch.
  • Partner logos are images. The organisation's name is given in the image's text alternative.
  • The London and Shanghai clocks on the contact page update every 30 seconds. Screen readers are not interrupted when they change.
  • We link to Google Maps and Baidu Maps instead of embedding a map. Those sites, the Global Innovator Awards ticket site, NEXUS Startup Diaries and UK Endorsing Services are run by others and are not covered by this statement.
  • Forms are not yet connected to our systems in this draft: a submitted form shows a confirmation, but nothing is sent.
  • Reports such as Doing Business in China 2027 may be published as PDFs. [Accessibility of downloadable documents to confirm]
