PART 1: Understand the business (read this before touching any tool)

1.1 NEXUS in one sentence

NEXUS is a London-based company that helps technology businesses from China (and the wider world) set up and grow in the UK, acts as the UK "office" for Chinese science parks and innovation zones, takes UK companies the other way into China and Hong Kong (delegations, partner representation), and helps UK tech scale-ups solve specific growth problems.

Think of NEXUS as a bridge with a team standing on it. Trade bodies and government point at the bridge and give advice. NEXUS walks people across and stays with them on the other side. Hence "boots on the ground" and "one point of accountability".

The bridge carries traffic both ways. The prototype calls it "a working corridor in both directions": - Inbound (China/Asia → UK): Chinese institutions and tech companies landing in the UK. This is the main revenue focus. - Outbound (UK → China/Asia): UK companies taking their first step into Asia via Hong Kong, RidgeCore representing NEXUS clients in China, 2027 delegations to mainland China and Hong Kong, and the Doing Business in China 2027 report (written for UK technology companies looking at China).

In March 2026 NEXUS opened a company in China with partners in Beijing and Shanghai (prototype timeline; wording to be confirmed). SEO must cover both directions, and the content plans in this guide do.

PART 3: SEO, AEO and GEO explained (with NEXUS examples)

3.1 The three kinds of "being found"

What it is Where it happens NEXUS example
SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) Ranking in the blue links Google, Bing (and Baidu in China) Ranking #3 for "UK soft landing programme"
AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) Being the direct answer Google featured snippets, "People also ask", voice The FAQ answer "What is a UK soft landing?" shown at the top of Google
GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) Being named or cited by AI chatbots ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Copilot, DeepSeek ChatGPT listing NEXUS when asked "who helps Chinese tech firms expand to the UK?"

They share 80% of the same work: clear pages, a clear entity and credible mentions. The differences are in emphasis.

3.2 How Google decides (the 4 pillars, in NEXUS terms)

  1. Crawl and index (technical): can Google reach and read the page? NEXUS today: no sitemap, no canonicals, 404s.
  2. Relevance (content): does the page match the search? NEXUS today: every title says "Nexus"; no service pages exist.
  3. Authority (links and mentions): do trusted sites vouch for it? NEXUS today: strong (884 domains), the one thing working.
  4. Experience (E-E-A-T + UX): Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust. Who wrote it, is it real, does the page load fast? NEXUS has strong raw material (1,400+ plans assessed, real MOUs, named founders) but none of it is marked up or proven on the page.

For NEXUS, B2B + international + money decisions = Google treats it as high-stakes. Trust signals (real address, company number, named team, case studies with consent, partner logos with permission) are ranking factors, not decoration.

3.3 How AI assistants decide (what the evidence says, Oct 2026)

From our Olostep research (raw/answer_geo_evidence.json): - Third-party mentions are repeatedly reported as the main signal for ChatGPT recommendations. AI trusts what others say about NEXUS (CBBC, City of London, GIA coverage, partner sites, press). - Copilot uses Bing's index: if Bing hasn't indexed a page, Copilot can't cite it. ChatGPT search also leans on Bing. So Bing Webmaster Tools matters a lot for NEXUS. - Structured data and clear, extractable text are recommended (no proof they work alone, but they remove ambiguity, which is NEXUS's biggest AI problem). - llms.txt: an SE Ranking study of ~300,000 domains (15 Oct 2026) found no link between having the file and being cited. Don't prioritise it. (The brief already says "no special AI files".) - Wikipedia/Wikidata, Reddit, Quora appear in AI source mixes. A Wikidata entry for NEXUS is a reasonable low-effort step; Wikipedia requires independent press coverage first.

For NEXUS specifically: the AI answer confused NEXUS with another "Nexus". The fix is entity consistency: same name, same one-line description, same address, same CEO, same links on the website, LinkedIn, Companies House, CBBC listing, GIA site, Crunchbase, Wikidata and partner sites.

3.4 The China dimension (important and often missed)

  • Google is blocked in mainland China. Chinese decision-makers use Baidu, WeChat (official accounts and search), Zhihu (expert Q&A), Xiaohongshu/RedNote, and Chinese AI (DeepSeek, Doubao, Kimi). Bing (cn.bing.com) works in China.
  • Evidenced Chinese terms for UK expansion: 英国市场准入 (UK market access), 英国公司注册 (UK company registration), 英国出海 (UK expansion), 英国公司设立 (UK company establishment). No volume data yet.
  • Chinese B2B buyers check AI summaries, community discussion, media mentions and case studies before visiting a company site.
  • What this means for NEXUS: 1. The new site must load in mainland China (already in the brief: no Google Fonts/Maps/reCAPTCHA/YouTube, Asia CDN). 2. Bing matters doubly (Copilot + China access). 3. Phase 2: a Chinese-language version (the brief allows a switcher), a WeChat official account and Zhihu answers. Partners (RidgeCore, TEDA, S-Tron) publishing about NEXUS in Chinese are worth more than anything on the English site. 4. Don't over-invest in Baidu SEO for an English .com site: Baidu favours Chinese-language, China-hosted sites. Use partner channels instead.

5.1 Google Search Console (GSC): "What Google sees and what people search to find us"

What it is: Google's free dashboard for site owners. The only source of real Google search data for NEXUS. Key concepts: - Impressions = times NEXUS appeared in results; Clicks; CTR = clicks ÷ impressions; Average position. - Query = the exact words typed. Brand vs non-brand = with or without "nexus". Non-brand growth is the main SEO goal. - Indexing: pages Google stored. "Crawled – currently not indexed" = seen but judged not worth keeping. - Domain property (verified via a DNS TXT record) covers www, non-www, http and https. Choose this over URL-prefix.

Set up now: 1. NEXUS adds a Domain property for nexuscreativehq.com (DNS TXT) and adds us as Full user. 2. Export Performance → last 16 months (queries + pages) = the pre-migration baseline. 3. Export Pages → Not indexed.

At launch: submit /sitemap.xml; URL Inspection → Request indexing for Home, the 3 audience pages and /asia-corridor; check Pages daily for 404 spikes for 2 weeks. Monthly: Performance → Query doesn't contain "nexus" → by page. Ask: Is /international-companies getting impressions for "uk market entry"? Which queries have impressions but CTR < 1%? (Rewrite those titles.) Connects to: GA4, Bing (import), Ahrefs (verification), Looker Studio.

5.2 Bing Webmaster Tools: "Bing, Copilot, ChatGPT search, and China"

What it is: Microsoft's GSC. Extra-important for NEXUS: Copilot only cites pages in Bing's index; ChatGPT search draws on Bing; Bing works in mainland China. Key concepts: IndexNow pings Bing instantly when a page changes. The AI Performance report shows which pages are cited in Copilot/Bing AI and the grounding queries the AI used. Set up: Import from GSC → submit sitemap → enable IndexNow. Monthly: AI Performance → cited pages + grounding queries → turn those queries into FAQs and Insights. Limit: citations, not clicks; Microsoft AI only.

5.3 Ahrefs Webmaster Tools (free): "Which old pages carry our authority?"

What it is: free, owner-verified Ahrefs (real Ahrefs data, own site only). Key concepts: - Backlink = a link from another site. Referring domain = a unique linking website (NEXUS: 884). 10 links from one site = 1 referring domain. - DR (Domain Rating) = 0–100 authority (NEXUS: 34). UR = the same for one page. - Dofollow links pass authority; nofollow mostly don't. - Best by links = your pages ranked by linking sites. This is the redirect priority list.

Do for NEXUS: 1. Verify via GSC (instant). 2. Site Explorer → Pages → Best by links → export including 404s → every URL into the redirect map. 3. Referring domains → sort by DR → note the top 20 (gov, university, partner sites). Their target pages must redirect perfectly. 4. After launch: monthly Site Audit + Broken backlinks → fix with new 301s.

Optional paid: Ahrefs Lite ($129/month): competitor research, rank tracking, AI-mention checks. Worth it for 3 months post-launch.

If present, write an answer-first block. - Keyword Gap: terms competitors rank for and NEXUS doesn't.

Do for NEXUS: 1. Keyword Overview (UK) on the seed list in Part 7 → record volume, KD and intent in a sheet. 2. Keyword Magic Tool on "uk market entry", "soft landing uk", "expand to uk", "set up company uk from china", "science park uk partnership" → Questions filter → FAQ/Insight ideas. 3. 5.5 Screaming Frog SEO Spider (free ≤ 500 URLs): "Is every page built right?"

What it is: a desktop crawler that visits every page like Googlebot and lists status, title, H1, canonical, meta, links and images. Key concepts: status codes (200 OK; 301 permanent redirect, which passes authority; 302 temporary, don't use; 404 not found; 5xx server error); redirect chain (A→B→C, slow and leaky; must be A→C); canonical (the page's "official" URL); indexability. Do for NEXUS: 1. Crawl staging (password-protected: Configuration → Authentication) → fix: missing/duplicate titles, ≠1 H1, missing meta, missing canonical, 404 links, images without alt. 2. List mode → upload all 175 old URLs (from live-site/page-inventory.csv + Ahrefs) → after launch, every row must be one 301 → a 200 page. No chains, no 404s. 3. Save the exports as acceptance evidence (brief §13). Tip: this is the tool that found "all 164 pages titled Nexus". Run it on the new site before sign-off.

5.6 PageSpeed Insights / Lighthouse (+ China speed test): "Is it fast and accessible?"

Key concepts (Core Web Vitals): LCP (main content loaded, < 2.5 s); INP (response to a tap, < 200 ms); CLS (layout jumping, < 0.1). Lighthouse scores 0–100 for performance, accessibility, best practice and SEO. Do for NEXUS: test every template on mobile. Targets: performance ≥ 85 (Home + audience pages), accessibility ≥ 95. Plus a mainland China speed test (e.g. a China-node uptime/speed service): Home and /asia-corridor load in < 5 s. Why: Chinese institutions are a core audience, and one Google Font or YouTube embed can make a page hang in China.
