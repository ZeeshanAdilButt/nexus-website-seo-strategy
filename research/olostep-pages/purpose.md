# OUR PURPOSE

## WHAT WE DO & HOW WE WORK

**WHAT WE DO**

NEXUS is a UK-based innovation hub, operating at the intersection of frontier technology, international market entry, and UK scale-up support.

Our mission is practical: we connect ambitious businesses, incubators, science parks, and innovation ecosystems across borders — and we help UK scale-ups grow through expert-led, project-based delivery.

We are not a generic accelerator. We are more than a founder membership club. We are the operational link between serious commercial ambition and the right market, partnership, or institutional connection.

China is our flagship international corridor. The UK is our home market. We hold formal government endorsement for the Innovator Founder visa route (through UKES), and we are a UK Government Supplier organisation.  
 

**HOW WE WORK** 

NEXUS operates through three service lines. Each is designed for a specific client type and delivered through a project-based model, with fractional expertise brought in for specialist briefs.

**1\. Cross-Border Ecosystem Bridge:** 

We act as the operational UK or China presence for international innovation institutions. For organisations based outside the UK, we provide UK-side representation, showcase events, deal-flow pipeline development, and expansion support into the UK market. For organisations seeking China access, we deploy through our live China corridor, in partnership with our collaborating organisation in China, RidgeCore Ventures. Our coverage here include:

*   Cross-border events and exhibition presence
*   UK and China establishment support
*   Overseas delegation visits and inbound hosting
*   Business plan assessment for UK market entry via the Innovator Founder Visa route

**Who Is This For:** Founders looking to establish a business in the UK. Additionally, science parks, incubators, innovation hubs, universities, government-linked innovation bodies, and institutional partners that need structured, operational UK or China connectivity — not consultancy reports.

**2\. Market Entry & Representation:** 

Entering a new market without a credible local presence is one of the most common and costly mistakes international companies make. NEXUS acts as boots on the ground. We work closely with cities, science parks, and our ecosystem partner network to serve our clients' interests in the UK and China.

*   Market intelligence reports
*   Supplier engagement
*   Franchise and expansion opportunities
*   Qualified lead generation and introductions
*   Investment raising support

**Who Is This For:** Companies entering a new market that need credible local presence, qualified introductions, and operational support, rather than an introduction and a contact list.

**3: UK Scale-Up Advisory & Partner Network:** 

NEXUS delivers expert-led, scoped projects that address the precise challenge you face. We bring specialist expertise for defined projects, and connect clients into our professional partner network for ongoing support beyond the NEXUS engagement. This includes:

*   AI strategy and adoption  
*   R&D tax credit solutions
*   Investment fundraising \[Seed, Series A\]
*   Roundtable and peer learning events
*   Project-based strategic support
*   Introductions to our professional partner and mentor network

**Who Is This For:** UK-based SMEs and scale-ups in frontier technology \[AI, deep tech, life sciences, advanced manufacturing, consumer tech\], that have a specific, defined brief and want expert-led delivery with clear outputs.
