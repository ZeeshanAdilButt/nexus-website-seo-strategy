The homepage looks like a competent wireframe wearing brand colours. It has plenty of emphasis, but little identity. NEXUS’s strongest asset—the working relationship between two markets—is reduced to a small dashboard beside a generic growth slogan.

Assessment based on all four images, the HTML source and the three supplied documents. No files modified.

## 1. What is wrong

**Six design problems**

1. **The typography is off-brand.** The source uses Archivo and Manrope instead of Neutral Face Bold and General Sans. The wide, heavy headlines create a different identity before anything else happens.
2. **Everything shouts equally.** Hero, process, results, events and insights repeat oversized uppercase statements. There is no quieter register to make the important moments exceptional.
3. **The hero’s composition is unbalanced.** Five lines of headline dominate; the corridor is squeezed into nested boxes. The faint background X and yellow underline feel added afterwards.
4. **The component system is generic.** Pills, rounded cards, outlined cards and tiny badges repeat everywhere. A partnership, a city, a service and an event all receive similar visual treatment.
5. **The page advertises its missing assets.** Empty photographs, seven blank portraits, an unapproved quotation and permission notes occupy prime space. This design depends on material that does not exist yet.
6. **The journey keeps restarting.** Audience tabs, corridor, process, partners and another route selector repeatedly explain the organisation. Alternating black/white/grey slabs create length without narrative progression.

**Five SEO/AEO problems**

1. **Query match is indirect.** The H1 names China and the UK but omits technology and market entry. “Who are you?” and “What we have delivered” add little topical meaning.
2. **Entity signals arrive late.** “NEXUS Creative HQ” should appear in the opening definition. The footer has useful legal details, but the supplied source lacks Organization structured data.
3. **Links do not deliver their promises.** Case studies point to `#top`; insights link to their own section; “How we count these numbers” goes to the team. These provide neither supporting evidence nor useful page discovery.
4. **Commercial substance is shallow.** Services sit inside a scripted selector. FAQs exist—good—but diagnostic deliverables, institutional representation and outbound support need clearer, directly accessible explanations.
5. **The publishing foundations are unfinished.** The title is “NEXUS homepage”; no description or canonical appears in the supplied source. Client-stated statistics and unresolved relationships are presented beside verified facts without sufficient qualification.

## 2. Creative concept: **The Crossing**

Make the brand’s X the organising principle: two directions meeting at one accountable operating point. An oversized, precisely cropped X anchors an editorial composition; its diagonal axes connect London with documented relationships in China. Yellow identifies the active connection through small markers and fine rules. Typography, relationship captions and evidence provide the imagery until real photography exists. The result should feel like an international institution with operational energy: spare, authoritative and specific. Use the approved X asset without distorting the wordmark.

## 4. Typography, rhythm and motion

- Self-host both brand fonts. Display: 72–88px desktop, 40–48px mobile; H2: 40–48/28–34px. Body: 18/17px, 1.5–1.6 line height.
- Uppercase Neutral Face for major headings only. General Sans sentence case for navigation, questions, descriptions and controls.
- Use 120px section spacing desktop, 64px mobile; 24–32px internal gaps; 55–70-character reading widths. Keep black dominant; yellow restricted to accents.
- **Crossing:** one brief line trace on entry; static with reduced motion.
- **Relationships:** focus/click highlights a connection and its caption; never hover-only.
- **Navigation:** understated underline movement and visible focus; no scroll hijacking or looping marquee.

## 5. Cut

Remove the city ticker, decorative underline, empty media, invented founder quote, unsupported clearance claim, repetitive process slogan and duplicate audience selector. Move the full team, specialist directory and visa explanation to dedicated pages. Publish fewer proof claims, each with a real destination.
