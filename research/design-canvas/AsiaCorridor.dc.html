Our Asia corridor, led by China.

Questions

The corridor, answered.

Who connects Tianjin and Shanghai technology companies with the UK?+

NEXUS Creative HQ, from London. NEXUS has two signed MOUs in Tianjin to bring Tianjin companies to the UK, and has exhibited and spoken at S-Tron Shanghai in 2025 and 2026.

How can a UK technology company enter China via Hong Kong?+

Many UK companies use Hong Kong as a familiar, international first base in Asia. NEXUS runs delegations to Hong Kong and mainland China, and RidgeCore Ventures represents NEXUS clients in China.

Does the corridor work in both directions?+

Yes. Chinese companies and institutions land in the UK through NEXUS, and UK companies reach China and Hong Kong through NEXUS delegations and partners.
