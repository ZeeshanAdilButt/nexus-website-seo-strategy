NEXUS Masterclass:  Innovator Founder ILR & Extensions Masterclass: Avoid the Pitfalls Before You Apply
Date & Time:
🗓️ November 26th, 2025 | ⏰ 2:00 PM (London)
Hosted by:
NEXUS Creative HQ
Featuring:
Barrister Tom Bradford
, Immigration Law Expert
Overview:
Preparing your
Innovator Founder ILR (Indefinite Leave to Remain)
or
visa extension
application can be one of the most pivotal — and challenging — moments in your founder journey. A single missing document or unclear milestone can lead to costly delays or refusals.
In this
exclusive NEXUS Masterclass
, Barrister
Tom Bradford
brings over 20 years of experience helping founders, investors, and startups navigate the UK immigration system successfully. He’ll break down what really matters when you apply — and how to avoid the common mistakes that derail even the most promising applications.
What You’ll Learn:
✅
Master the Milestones
– How to present your
significant achievements
and meet the “greater than mean” success test with clarity and confidence.
✅
Get Your Evidence Right
– Understand what proof is required for IP, R&D, and cumulative absences — and how to structure it effectively.
✅
Insider Guidance from Experience
– Practical insights and real examples from 20+ years of working with founders, helping you build a rock-solid application and stay one step ahead.
Who Should Attend:
Innovator Founder visa holders approaching ILR or extension
Startup founders planning for long-term settlement in the UK
Advisors and consultants supporting founders with immigration and business growth
Why Attend:
This masterclass isn’t just about compliance — it’s about
strategy
. You’ll gain the clarity, structure, and insight needed to make your application stand out for the right reasons.
