NEXUS Representing The UK At S-Tron Shanghai 2025
🇬🇧✨ We are proud to announce that
NEXUS
will represent the UK at S-Tron Shanghai 2025 (formerly Slush China) next week, as the festival marks its 10th anniversary.
We’ll be hosting the
NEXUS
× THT Innovation Pavilion, where we lead a U.K delegation of NEXUS member businesses, spotlighting UK–China collaboration and showcasing startups across AI, life sciences, creative tech, and advanced manufacturing.
S-Tron China (S创中国)
brings together over 13,000 attendees, 1,500+ investors, 200+ speakers, 200+ exhibitors, and 300+ startup pitches across six innovation tracks. We’re looking forward to joining an international line-up including
Airwallex
,
Huawei Cloud
and
Cyan Robotics
who will be showcasing at the event. For the full list of exhibitors see:
https://lnkd.in/g2iBr_K9
