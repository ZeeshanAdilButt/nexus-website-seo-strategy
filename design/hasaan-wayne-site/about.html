About NEXUS Creative HQ

One accountable team in London.

NEXUS helps technology businesses land, grow and scale in the UK, with China as its flagship corridor.

We are the UK growth partner for international technology companies, the innovation institutions that back them, and UK scale-ups ready for their next stage. Hong Kong is our gateway to the wider region, with partners on the ground in China.
