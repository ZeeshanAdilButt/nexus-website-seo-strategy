What we do

NEXUS Creative HQ is a consortium partner of UK Endorsing Services (UKES). In that role we review Innovator Founder visa applications as part of the UKES endorsement process.

The Innovator Founder route is for people who want to set up an innovative business in the UK. Applicants need an endorsement from an approved endorsing body before they apply for the visa. An endorsement is not a visa: the decision on the visa itself is made by the Home Office.

Separate from our commercial services

This work is entirely separate from our commercial services. Buying a NEXUS service has no bearing on an endorsement decision, and endorsement cannot be bought.

How to apply

Applications are made through UK Endorsing Services, not through NEXUS. If you are applying, start on the UKES website. If you need immigration advice, we can introduce you to immigration law specialists.

If you have a question about an application already in progress, contact UK Endorsing Services directly.
