Common questions

Insights on doing business in China→
Can NEXUS represent my company in China?

Yes, through our reciprocal partnership with RidgeCore Ventures. We handle UK representation for their clients, and they handle China representation for ours. Together we open access to investment, knowledge transfer and franchise opportunities.

Why is Hong Kong part of the corridor?

Hong Kong is a familiar, international base for UK companies taking their first step into Asia, and a launch point for Chinese companies heading to the UK and Europe.

Am I committing to anything when I register interest in a 2027 delegation?

No. Registering interest carries no commitment. We share the programme and costs when they are confirmed. Places are limited for each trip.

Where will the 2027 delegations go, and what will they cost?

Do you also help companies from China enter the UK?

Yes. We work with technology companies entering the UK, and with the science parks, innovation zones and funds that back them. See UK market entry for international companies and UK partnerships for institutions. UK companies already trading can start with a growth diagnostic for UK scale-ups.
