Common questions

Straight answers.

What is NEXUS Creative HQ?+

NEXUS Creative HQ is a London-based growth partner and the operational bridge between the UK and China for technology companies, science parks and innovation zones. One accountable team in London works with partners on the ground in Tianjin, Shanghai and Hong Kong.

Who does NEXUS work with?+

Three groups: innovation institutions that need a UK presence for their portfolio, international technology companies entering the UK, and UK frontier-tech scale-ups with a specific growth problem.

How does working with NEXUS start?+

Companies start with a paid diagnostic; institutions start with a partnership call. Pricing is on application.

Does NEXUS have partners in China?+

Yes. RidgeCore Ventures represents NEXUS clients in China under a reciprocal partnership. NEXUS has two signed MOUs in Tianjin and has exhibited and spoken at S-Tron Shanghai.

Do you help with Innovator Founder visas?+

NEXUS reviews and endorses applications as a consortium partner of UK Endorsing Services (UKES). This is separate from our commercial services. Read about our endorsing role.
