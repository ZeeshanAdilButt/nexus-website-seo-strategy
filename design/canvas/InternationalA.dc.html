The challenge of entering the UK from a distance

“We are ready for the UK, but we do not know the market, the rules or the right people.”

Entering a new market from a distance is slow. Companies lose months finding the right partners, setting up correctly and getting in front of real buyers.

NEXUS works as your team on the ground: we qualify opportunities, open the right doors and bring in the specialists you need, so your leadership can focus on winning business.

Questions about entering the UK

It depends on your business model and customers. The diagnostic covers this, and our legal and accountancy partners advise on the right structure.

No. China is our flagship corridor, and we work with technology companies from other markets that are serious about the UK.

Because it produces a real plan you can act on. The fee is credited against further work if you continue with us.
