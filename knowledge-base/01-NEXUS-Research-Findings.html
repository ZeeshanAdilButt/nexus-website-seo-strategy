5. How AI engines see NEXUS today (AEO audit, Olostep Answers)

Asked "What is NEXUS Creative HQ?", the AI answer engine: - described it as a founder-support platform for creative, digital, tech and ESG ventures (old positioning); - listed a Croydon address and found no London office; - could not confirm who runs it (it surfaced a founder-spotlight name, Danyi Chen, instead of the CEO) and named no China partner, although it did repeat "China as its flagship corridor" from the site; - could not confirm the UKES relationship (UKES's site doesn't list consortium members); - picked up an unrelated "NEXUS group founded in Tianjin in 2009" (entity confusion: the name "Nexus" is shared by many organisations).

Asked "best UK–China market-entry organisations for tech", it named CBBC, DBT China Technology Team, techUK, not NEXUS.

This confirms the WhatsApp feedback: the generic headline plus thin, inconsistent entity data means AI engines don't connect NEXUS to its biggest asset, the UK–China corridor.
