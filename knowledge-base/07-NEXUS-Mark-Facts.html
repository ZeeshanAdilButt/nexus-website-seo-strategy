  1. Order of authority: Brand Guidelines 2025, then the Developer Brief, then the prototype copy, then everything else. The brief itself says "where the prototype differs from the guidelines, the guidelines win".

9. Forms and tracking

Every form must: - validate input - save the submission to the site database (staff can view, filter and export CSV) - email the right inbox within 5 minutes - send an acknowledgement - show a thank-you message - record the source fields

Also: - A JSON webhook for the future CRM, built in Phase 1 and switched off. - Spam protection: a honeypot plus rate limiting. No Google reCAPTCHA (it is blocked in mainland China). - Diagnostics in Phase 1 are a request form only. NEXUS replies by hand with a booking and payment link.

Field keys: - form_type takes one of: partnership_call, diagnostic_international, diagnostic_scaleup, sponsor_pack, delegation_interest, event_interest, general, report_download. - Other fields: name, email, organisation, role, country, organisation_type, sector, stage, timeline, interest, budget_band, message, marketing_consent (never pre-ticked). - Hidden: page_url, cta, utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, referrer, landing_page, submitted_at.

Analytics events (after consent only): - Primary: partnership_call_request, diagnostic_request, sponsor_pack_request - Conversions: delegation_interest, event_interest, general_enquiry - Secondary: report_download - Tracked: cta_click, case_study_view, outbound_gia_click

Success is measured by qualified enquiries, not traffic.
