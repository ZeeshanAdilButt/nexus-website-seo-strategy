# NEXUS Networking for Creative, Tech and Digital Founders

We are thrilled to invite you to attend the launch of our monthly [NEXUS](/ "https://nexuscreativehq.com/") creative, tech and digital business networking events—with a delicious ingredient! 🎭✨

Join us on Wednesday 7th of May at 5pm at the stunning city located private members club, [Blue Marlin Ibiza London](https://bluemarlinibiza.london/ "https://bluemarlinibiza.london/").

Whether you attend to explore new creative horizons, dive into the latest in tech or connect with like-minded innovators our [NEXUS](/ "https://nexuscreativehq.com/") networking events are about serendipitous encounters that will evolve to being your next clients, suppliers, co-founders or investment opportunities.

In partnership, the launch event finds [NEXUS](/ "https://nexuscreativehq.com/") and [Women in Finance](https://www.womeninfinancegroup.com/ "https://www.womeninfinancegroup.com") curating a space for bold ideas to thrive—where the precision of finance meets the passion of sport and the power of creativity. Discussions aim to redefine leadership by highlighting how athletic mindset, creative thinking, and financial acumen intersect to drive progress, empower women, and build resilient, dynamic teams.

In addition to networking, we invite you to extend your evening to the next level with an exclusively curated [salt&c supper club](https://www.saltandc.com/event-details/beats-bites "https://www.saltandc.com/event-details/beats-bites").

This intimate dining experience will be an evening of Italian flavours with a live DJ.
