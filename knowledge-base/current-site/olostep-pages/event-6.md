# NEXUS Representing The UK At S-Tron Shanghai 2025

*   [![linkedin-b](https://nexuscreativehq.com/img/icons/social/linkedin-b.svg)](https://www.linkedin.com/sharing/share-offsite/?url=https://nexuscreativehq.com/event/6)

🇬🇧✨ We are proud to announce that [NEXUS](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nexuscreativehq/) will represent the UK at S-Tron Shanghai 2025 (formerly Slush China) next week, as the festival marks its 10th anniversary.  
  
We’ll be hosting the [NEXUS](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nexuscreativehq/) × THT Innovation Pavilion, where we lead a U.K delegation of NEXUS member businesses, spotlighting UK–China collaboration and showcasing startups across AI, life sciences, creative tech, and advanced manufacturing.  
  
[S-Tron China (S创中国)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/s-tronchina/) brings together over 13,000 attendees, 1,500+ investors, 200+ speakers, 200+ exhibitors, and 300+ startup pitches across six innovation tracks. We’re looking forward to joining an international line-up including [Airwallex](https://www.linkedin.com/company/airwallex/), [Huawei Cloud](https://www.linkedin.com/company/huaweicloudservice/) and [Cyan Robotics](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cyanrobotics/) who will be showcasing at the event. For the full list of exhibitors see: [https://lnkd.in/g2iBr\_K9](https://lnkd.in/g2iBr_K9)  
  
More news on participating [NEXUS](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nexuscreativehq/) member companies will be announced this week...  
  
📍 Where: West Bund Art Center, Hall A  
📅 When: September 23–24, 2025
