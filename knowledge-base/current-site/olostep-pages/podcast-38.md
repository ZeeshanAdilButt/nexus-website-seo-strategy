*   What does it really take to build a startup that changes the game for underrepresented creatives? In this episode of NEXUS Talks, host Deana Soni sits down with Adaora O\-Njoku, CEO & Founder of Amaka Studio — a platform connecting brands with Black and African creative talent worldwide. Adaora opens up about the real struggles of fundraising, pivoting, and staying true to your mission, while also sharing raw insights on what success looks like (and why she still doesn’t feel “successful”).
