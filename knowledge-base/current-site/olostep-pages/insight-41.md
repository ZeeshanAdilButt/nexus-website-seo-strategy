This International Women’s Month, we’re celebrating trailblazing women founders who are driving innovation across industries—from FinTech and sustainability to healthcare and AI. 🌍✨  
  
Here are 6 women-led startups making waves in 2025:  
  
[Luna Daily](https://www.linkedin.com/company/luna-daily/) – Founded by [Katy Cottam](https://www.linkedin.com/in/katy-cottam-b6556043/), redefining feminine hygiene and breaking taboos around women’s health.

[Tyms](https://www.linkedin.com/company/tyms-io/) – Led by [Chineye Ochem MBA, ACA, CFE](https://www.linkedin.com/in/chineyeochem/), simplifying financial management for African businesses with AI-powered bookkeeping.  
  
[Econic Technologies Ltd](https://www.linkedin.com/company/econic-technologies-limited/) – Founded by Charlotte Williams, pioneering CO₂-based materials to reduce industrial carbon footprints.  
  
[Acurable](https://www.linkedin.com/company/acurable/) – Founded by [Esther Rodriguez-Villegas](https://www.linkedin.com/in/esther-rodriguez-villegas-5241bb4/), revolutionizing sleep apnea monitoring with a portable health tech device.  
  
[Incrediballs](https://www.linkedin.com/company/incrediballs/) – Founded by [Brianne West](https://www.linkedin.com/in/briannemwest/), tackling plastic waste in the beverage industry with soda tablets.  
  
[Oova](https://www.linkedin.com/company/oova-inc/) – Founded by [Amy](https://www.linkedin.com/company/amy/) Divaraniya leveraging AI-driven hormone tracking to support women’s reproductive health.  
  
These women are \*\*disrupting industries, solving global challenges, and redefining what’s possible.\*\* 💡🚀
