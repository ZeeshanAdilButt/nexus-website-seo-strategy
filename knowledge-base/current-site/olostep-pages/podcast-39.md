In this episode of NEXUS Talks, we sit down with Ashley Ainsley, co-founder of Colour in Tech, to uncover the real truth about building partnerships with big brands — and why it can take years, not months, to land the right deal. From being one of only 32 Black students at Oxford to building a movement that’s transforming diversity in tech, Ashley shares his journey, the challenges he’s faced, and the strategies that have helped him secure game-changing collaborations.  

You’ll hear how he’s:  

*   Navigated the realities of running a sustainable nonprofit  
    

*   Landed multi-year partnerships that took four years to secure  
    

*   Used diversity and inclusion as a driver for innovation  
    

*   Taken AI training nationwide to up-skill thousands
