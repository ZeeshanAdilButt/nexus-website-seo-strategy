🍴 From Hobby Cook to Netflix Chef: The Rise of MsJoyceK | NEXUS Talks 🎙️ Episode Overview In this episode of NEXUS Talks, we sit down with MsJoyceK — the self-taught private chef, food entrepreneur, and digital content creator known for her creative recipes, warm personality, and viral TikTok cooking videos. What started as a hobby in her family kitchen has become an extraordinary journey spanning continents, cuisines, and careers. From a two-year adventure in Congo, where she launched her first food venture, to cooking for high-profile clients in London, Joyce has turned her love of food into a thriving business and personal brand. Now, after appearing on Netflix’s Five Star Chef, she joins host Deana Soni to share her story of creativity, consistency, and courage — and what it really takes to build a sustainable career doing what you love. --- 🌍 In This Episode \* How MsJoyceK went from posting simple home recipes online to becoming one of the UK’s most recognised private chefs and content creators \* The turning point that led to her first chef role at The Ivy, one of London’s most iconic restaurants \* Behind the scenes of her experience competing on Netflix’s Five Star Chef \* How travel and culture shape her food, from London to Congo and beyond \* The challenges of balancing passion with business as a creative entrepreneur \* Her best advice for aspiring chefs, content creators, and creative founders --- 🔥 Why You’ll Love This Episode If you’ve ever dreamed of turning your creative passion into a business, this episode is a masterclass in possibility. Joyce opens up about: \* Building a personal brand rooted in authenticity and culture \* Staying consistent on social media while avoiding burnout \* Creating content that connects with audiences on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube \* Managing the business side of being a private chef — from client relationships to taxes and brand deals \* Using food as a tool for connection, storytelling, and empowerment Her journey shows that you don’t need formal training or big budgets to make an impact — just creativity, courage, and a willingness to start. --- 🍳 About MsJoyceK Joyce — known online as @MsJoyceK — is a UK-based private chef, celebrity chef, and culinary consultant celebrated for her vibrant flavours and creative recipes. She built her brand from the ground up, using platforms like TikTok and Instagram to share easy, approachable dishes that bring people together. During the pandemic, her simple “restaurant-style” recipes went viral, helping millions rediscover the joy of cooking at home. Since then, she’s catered for global brands, hosted supper clubs, and appeared as a contestant on Netflix’s Five Star Chef, where her creativity and authenticity earned praise from world-renowned chefs, including Michel Roux Jr. --- 💬 Quote From The Episode “I’ve always loved cooking, but I never thought it would be my career. I started posting recipes online for fun — and that’s what led to my first chef job.
