Imposter Syndrome
Do you ever feel like you're a fraud? Like you don't deserve your success? You're not alone. Imposter syndrome is a common experience, especially among entrepreneurs and startup founders.
ㅤ
But there are things you can do to deal with imposter syndrome. Here are a few tips:
ㅤ
- Recognise that it's normal.
- Talk to someone about it.
- Focus on your strengths.
- Celebrate your successes.
- Don't be afraid to ask for help.
ㅤ
If you're a founder who's struggling with imposter syndrome, remember that you're not alone. And you can overcome it.
