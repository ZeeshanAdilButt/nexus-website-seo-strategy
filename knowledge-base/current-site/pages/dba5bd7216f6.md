Building a Global Community for Creatives
Klaus Heller, a social media marketing freelancer and founder of the Creative Lunch Club, discusses his journey in the industry and the changes he has witnessed over the years. He emphasises the importance of using social media strategically to achieve business goals and the need for a media budget. Klaus also shares insights on creating good content, including the importance of engaging the audience and being authentic. He talks about the motivation behind starting the Creative Lunch Club and the goal of building a global community for creatives. Klaus encourages aspiring freelancers to take the leap and start their own ventures.
