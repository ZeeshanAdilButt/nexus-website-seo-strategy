NEXUS Masterclass: Equity Crowdfunding 101

🎓 NEXUS MasterClass: Equity Crowdfunding 101 with Michael Wilkinson (Crowdcube)

 

📅 Date: Wednesday 30 July 2025

🕒 Time: 2:00 PM BST

📍 Location: Online (Free Registration Required)

 

Learn how to raise funding with the crowd from the expert behind Monzo’s record-breaking raise.

 

Are you a startup founder looking to raise investment without relying solely on VCs or angels? This live webinar is designed for B2C startups, community-driven brands, and early-stage founders ready to learn how equity crowdfunding can fuel growth.

 

Join us for Equity Crowdfunding 101, a free online MasterClass led by Michael Wilkinson, founding team member at Crowdcube — the platform behind Monzo’s iconic £1M raise in just 96 seconds.

 

Whether you’re planning your first raise or want to scale with the help of your community, this session will equip you with a practical roadmap for equity crowdfunding success.

 

What you’ll learn:

 

🔍 The UK startup funding landscape in 2025

🧩 Equity crowdfunding explained in plain English

⚠️ Common mistakes, surprising wins & realistic success rates

🗺️ A step-by-step 4-month crowdfunding roadmap

📑 Key investor terms every founder needs to know

 

Who should attend:

 

Startup founders planning their first or next funding round

B2C brands and product-based startups with engaged communities

Founders exploring alternative funding routes beyond traditional investors

 

Why this webinar is unmissable:

 

💬 “If you missed our last sold-out MasterClass, don’t make the same mistake twice.”
