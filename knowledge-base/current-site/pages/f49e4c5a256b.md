Nexus
NEXUS is the UK's operational bridge for frontier technology companies and innovation ecosystems. We connect ambitious businesses, incubators, science parks and scale-ups across borders, with China as our flagship international corridor. Domestically, NEXUS helps UK scale-ups grow through expert-led, project-based delivery.
WHAT WE DO
Cross-border ecosystem bridge, market entry and representation, and UK scale-up advisory — three service lines, one point of accountability.
