1. The project in brief
Who we are.
 NEXUS (NEXUS Creative HQ Ltd, company number 12667243) helps technology businesses land, grow and scale in the UK. Our main international corridor is China, within a wider Asia focus. We work with three groups of clients:
innovation institutions such as science parks, innovation zones, hubs and funds
international technology companies entering the UK
UK technology scale-ups that are already trading
Professional firms such as law, accountancy and banking firms partner with us and sponsor our events. We also review and endorse Innovator Founder visa applicants as a consortium partner of UK Endorsing Services (UKES). That work must always be kept separate from our commercial services on the site.
Why we are rebuilding.
 Our current site, www.nexuscreativehq.com, describes what we used to do rather than what we sell. It has no service pages, no clear routes for each type of client, no case studies and no proper enquiry routes. The new site has to explain our offer clearly, prove it, and turn the right visitors into enquiries.
What success looks like.
 A steady flow of qualified enquiries from each type of client, with the source of each one recorded: partnership calls, diagnostic requests, sponsor pack requests and delegation registrations.
Target launch.
 We want the new site to replace our current website before the Global Innovator Awards on 16 November 2026. Please aim to go live by Thursday 12 November, and tell us in your proposal whether that is achievable. If it is not, tell us what can go live by then; our priorities are in section 12. You will not need access to our current website: we will send you the list of current addresses, the existing articles, our content and access to our domain settings for the switchover.

9. Security, including the PHP vulnerabilities
On 21 October 2026 the NCSC issued an alert about two critical PHP vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619. The NCSC says they may let attackers execute commands on any web server running PHP, and that organisations using any version of PHP are affected (
NCSC alert
). The new site must be protected against these and any future vulnerabilities. 
Approved
What the new website must include
Inventory.
 Give us a written list of every component and its version: platform, language runtime (including PHP if used), plugins, libraries, server software and third-party services. Keep it updated.
Supported versions only.
 If PHP is used anywhere, run a currently supported PHP branch, patched to the latest fixed release at launch. No end-of-life software of any kind.
Smallest attack surface.
 Prefer pre-built pages, with form handling in one small, hardened endpoint. Remove unused plugins, extensions and features. Turn off error display on the live site. Give each component only the permissions it needs.
Patching commitment.
 Apply critical security updates within 48 hours of release, and other security updates within 14 days. Put this in the support agreement and tell us when it has been done.
Vendor monitoring.
 Watch security notices for every third-party product the site depends on, including any that bundle PHP, and apply their updates.
Protection layers.
 HTTPS everywhere with HSTS. A web application firewall or CDN protection. Security headers (Content-Security-Policy, X-Content-Type-Options, frame-ancestors, Referrer-Policy). Rate limiting on forms and the admin login.
Admin access.
 Strong unique passwords and two-factor authentication for every admin account. Admin area not publicly advertised. Accounts removed when people leave.
Data.
 Enquiry data encrypted in transit and at rest. Admin and database access logged. Backups protected.
Testing.
 Before launch, run a vulnerability scan and a dependency audit, fix every high or critical finding, and send us the results.
Monitoring.
 Uptime monitoring and alerts for unexpected file changes, with a named contact who responds to security incidents.
As a UK Government supplier with security clearance, we expect these controls to follow the NCSC's guidance and the Cyber Essentials standard. Please confirm in your proposal that they will.
